# Child Safety Standards — Phone (com.gr4nt.dialer)

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Gr4nt, the developer of Phone, has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation
(CSAE)** and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

## About the app

Phone is a phone dialer and contacts app. It doesn't provide social features, public profiles,
chat, media sharing, user-generated content or any way for strangers to find or contact each
other. Calls go through the user's mobile network, and the app has no internet access.

## Our standards

- The app must never be used to sexualise, groom, exploit or endanger children. Any such use is
  prohibited.
- We don't host, store or distribute content between users. Call recordings stay only on the
  recording user's own device.
- Any report of CSAE or CSAM connected to the app is treated as a priority. Where we receive
  information about apparent CSAM or a child at risk, we report it to the relevant authorities,
  including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and national law
  enforcement, as required by law.
- We cooperate with law enforcement requests made through proper legal channels.

## Reporting a concern in the app

In **Settings › Safety › Report a Child Safety Concern**, users can:

- email the developer directly;
- report child sexual abuse material to NCMEC's CyberTipline (<https://report.cybertip.org>);
- find how to contact local police or a child-protection hotline.

If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

## Compliance

The app complies with applicable child-safety laws, and we report confirmed CSAM to NCMEC and to
regional and national authorities as required.
